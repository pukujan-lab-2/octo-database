## Summary

Create a minimal operational model for background work so thumbnails, archive/restore, and later agent/data jobs are observable and retryable without relying on ad-hoc process logs.

## PDD / product goal

Every important background operation has a stable job identity, visible state, retry semantics, and audit/activity evidence. A worker interruption must not create duplicate destructive side effects.

## Success condition

- one worker processes queued work;
- dashboard shows queued/running/completed/failed;
- retry count/error summary are visible;
- archive or thumbnail job survives forced worker termination and converges correctly after restart;
- idempotency prevents multiple logical completions;
- authorized owner can inspect the operations page; delegated roles follow their owning slice.

## SDD / job model

Canonical job fields:
- id;
- workspace_id;
- job_type;
- state;
- idempotency_key;
- attempt;
- available_at / lease timestamps if needed;
- created/started/completed timestamps;
- error code/summary;
- created_by principal;
- related resource/run ID.

States should be explicit and monotonic where possible: queued→running→completed, with retryable failure returning through a controlled transition.

Activity/audit events are human-readable projections of actions, not substitutes for raw debug logs.

## Scope control

In scope:
- Postgres-backed job table;
- one worker;

- failure drill.

Out of scope:
