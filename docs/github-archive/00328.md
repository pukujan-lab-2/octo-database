## Summary

The old cut was a car floating on a grid — no city, no motion, nothing for the eye to track. This rebuild puts it in a street that streams past, so the speed is legible from the frame itself.

- The car is forged out of flying triangles, then drives.
- The city emits opaque tower faces alongside its wireframe, so towers occlude each other and the skyline reads as depth instead of additive haze.
- Two travelling highlights run over the body; lamp posts rake past the lens.
- World travel is a LUT integrated from the blended beat speeds, so the city never jitters when beats cross-fade.
- Sound is attempted on load and the first gesture anywhere unlocks it; starting muted meant nobody heard the narration shipped with the previous cut.

## Fixes found while rendering

- **Car rendered as a solid white mass** — the GLB stores one vertex per triangle corner, so `EdgesGeometry` treated every triangle edge as a boundary. Vertices are welded by position first.
- **Car went grey then magenta on the leak** — RGB lerp desaturates through grey and HSL swings through magenta. Replaced with explicit palette stops.
- **Car saturated to white** — additive blending summed thousands of overlapping edges. Base edges are now normal-blended.
- **A static bar across every frame** — a fixed-depth streak sprite and lamp flares that lit up while still a kilometre away. Lamps now flare as they pass the lens.
- **Bloom ate the wireframe** — the finished car is off the bloom layer; only the forge is on it.
