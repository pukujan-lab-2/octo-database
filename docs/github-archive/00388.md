# Issue #388: Convert media on upload (FLAC/Opus audio, WebP images, video re-encode)

- State: open
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-31
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/octo-database/issues/388

## Why

Media will be most of the bytes in R2. Today (Pro, 2026-10-31) R2 holds 4.5 GB: 3.5 GB MP4, 450 MB WAV, 238 MB PNG/JPEG.

## Work requested

A small worker step after upload, using FFmpeg and libvips:

- WAV to FLAC (lossless) or Opus.
- A WebP or AVIF display copy for images.
- Optional re-encode of large videos (H.265/AV1 at a sensible bitrate).

Keep the original where the workspace needs it.
