### Summary

Every guest login adds a principal and a workspace that are never removed. On the VPS, 19 guest principals and 16 guest workspaces had built up by 2026-11-01, and the workspace databases table lists each of them.

### Identity and Lineage

- related: #432 (the tables and key-use cleanup, where the guests showed up)

### Acceptance Criteria

1. An hourly sweep deletes guest workspaces and guest principals created more than 7 days ago.
2. A guest workspace that has files, a provisioned database or a key used in the last 7 days is left alone.
3. A unit test shows the sweep's two statements, and the hourly retention sweep calls it.

### Boundaries and Next Action

- No change to guest login, session length or limits.
- Next action: open the pull request.

### Symptoms

1. `select count(*) from octo.principals where is_guest` was 19 on 2026-11-01 with every session long expired.
2. The Workspace databases table showed 16 "Personal (Guest)" rows next to the owner's real workspaces.

### Hypotheses (unverified)

- **H1:** every old guest workspace is empty. Would refute: a guest with files or a database turns up in the check before the delete. The 2026-11-01 check found none.

### Counter-signal

Guest rows are tiny, so disk use is not a concern.

### Honest Caveat

The count comes from one look at the production database.

### Problems vs Gaps

- Problem: rows nothing ever deletes.
- Gap: no lifecycle for guest accounts.

### Proposal *(proposal)*

An hourly delete of guest workspaces then guest principals older than 7 days, skipping any with files, a database or a recently used key.
