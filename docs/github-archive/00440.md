Guest accounts and their workspaces were never deleted, so they piled up in the owner's lists.

## Problem and consequence

Every guest login adds a principal and an auto-provisioned workspace, and nothing removes them. On the VPS there were 19 guests and 16 guest workspaces by 2026-11-01, all listed in the Workspace databases table.

## What changes

- `src/server/db.ts`: `dbPruneGuests` deletes guest workspaces, then guest principals, created more than 7 days ago. A guest workspace with files, a provisioned database or a key used in the last 7 days is skipped, and so is its guest.
- `src/server/queue.ts`: the hourly sweep calls it and logs the counts.
- `tests/unit/prune-guests.test.ts`: the two statements run in order with the 7-day window, and the skip rules are in the workspace delete.

## Before and after

Before: guests stay forever.
After: empty guests older than 7 days are deleted every hour.

## What stays unchanged

Guest login, session length and limits. No migration. Guests with files or a database are never deleted.
