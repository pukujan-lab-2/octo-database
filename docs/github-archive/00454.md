Octo runs its own Sablier, though the VPS is to have exactly one, run by the host's `platform` stack.

## Problem and consequence

Sablier finds containers by label only, so a second instance on the host would stop the first one's containers. Octo's copy also keeps sessions in memory only, so every Octo deploy forgets them. The owner decided that server-management owns Sablier on the VPS (Pukujan-lab/server-management#85) and that idle services stop after 10 minutes.

## What changes

- `docker-compose.vps.yml`: the `sablier` service is gone. `octo-web` joins the external `sablier` network, which the `platform` stack creates.
- `Caddyfile`: `session_duration 10m` on the three Sablier routes.

## Before and after

Before: `octo-sablier` in Octo's project, Phoenix and the embedder stop after 30 idle minutes.
After: Octo's Caddy calls the shared Sablier at the same `http://sablier:10000`, and they stop after 10 idle minutes.

## How to verify

On the VPS, `docker compose -f docker-compose.yml -f docker-compose.edge.yml -f docker-compose.vps.yml config` lists no `sablier` service and shows `octo-web` on `sablier`. `caddy validate` in the running `octo-web` image says "Valid configuration". After deploy: `docker ps` shows only the `sablier` container, and Phoenix stops 10 minutes after its last request.

## What stays unchanged

Labels, health checks, blocking timeouts, the Caddy plugin and the `http://sablier:10000` address.
